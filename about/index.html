about

I’m a Machine Learning Engineer focused on production ML, data engineering, and applied AI research. I like turning messy, real-world problems into simple, reliable systems.

My goal with this site is to write clearly about the parts of ML that actually matter in practice. I dig into papers and implementations, chase the small details that change results, and share what I learn in plain language. Writing forces understanding; it’s how I stress-test ideas and make them useful.

I’m interested in deep learning across NLP, vision, and graph methods, and I care about the boring-but-critical bits: data quality, evaluation, observability, and shipping models that keep working after the launch blog post. Outside of AI, I enjoy finance, sports, and the occasional anime rabbit hole.
